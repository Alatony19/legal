Delete your Project6 account

Last updated: 25 July 2026 · App: Project6 (com.project6.run)

You can delete your Project6 account and its associated data at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1 — In the app

  1. Open Project6 and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm when prompted.

Your profile is hidden from other users immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2 — By email

If you can no longer sign in, email antonioalagrande@gmail.com from the address registered to your account with the subject “Project6 account deletion”. We will verify ownership and delete the account within 30 days.

What happens to your data

Removed immediately on request:

  • Your username, display name, avatar, bio, and location label.
  • Your body metrics (weight, height, birth year, sex).
  • Your profile stops being visible or searchable to other users.

Permanently erased after a 30-day grace period:

  • Your login credentials and account record.
  • Your recorded runs, GPS routes, and any health or fitness data imported from Health Connect or Apple Health.
  • Tracks you published, crew memberships, event registrations, messages, comments, kudos, photos, and all other content tied to your account.

Grace period. For 30 days after deletion you can restore your account by simply signing back in with the same credentials and choosing to restore. After 30 days the erasure is permanent and cannot be undone.

Retained. We keep nothing tied to you after the grace period. Aggregate, non-identifying statistics (for example total distance run across all users) may persist because they cannot be linked back to you. Records we are legally required to keep are retained only as long as the law requires.
